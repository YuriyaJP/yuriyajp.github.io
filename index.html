Published Research

Cost-Effectiveness of Youth Suicide Prevention in Japan

Published on Effective Altruism Forum, 2025

Quantitative evaluation modeling intervention impact using DALYs averted, cost per life saved, and sensitivity analysis. Scraped data from 24+ fragmented government data sources (National Police Agency reports) to build cost-effectiveness estimates for hotline services, school outreach, and community workshops.

Methods: Fermi estimation, data extraction from PDFs using Python, analysed yearly trends and sources of suicide per age group, regional disparities
Key Approach: Modeled three intervention types (hotlines, school programs, workshops) and their impact, calculated cost per DALY under multiple persistence assumptions
