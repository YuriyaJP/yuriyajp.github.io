Hi! I'm Yulia.

I design evaluation systems and cost-effectiveness models that help organisations make better decisions with imperfect data.

2017 - I earned a BA in psycholinguistics, researching how societies respond to major socioeconomic shifts from the 1950s to today. I analysed how economic transitions reshape language and how entire communication patterns evolve under pressure.

2021 - MA in Computational Linguistics (Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf).

2022 - Double MA in Statistical Methods in Behavioural Science (Chiba University). Trained in causal inference: separating signal from correlation through experimental methods, observational analysis, and behavioural feedback loops.

2024 - Global Voyage on a cruise ship. Delivered over 50 workshops on nonproliferation of nuclear weapons, military propaganda, humanitarian aid etc to organisations like the Chatham House (UK), UN Headquarters (New York), Rafto Foundation (Norway) Universidad de Colombia (Colombia) and others.

2025 - Intensive Data Science & AI training (Le Wagon Tokyo).

2025 - Partnerships & Operations Manager at a nonprofit, Tokyo. I built monitoring & evaluation systems from scratch, designed cost-effectiveness models, and managed a grant portfolio.

2025 - Published cost-effectiveness research on youth suicide prevention. I worked with incomplete government data, using Fermi estimation and explicit uncertainty modelling to produce decision-relevant insights in low-data environments.

2026 onward - I started individual consultancy to help organisations make high-stakes decisions and best strategy for impact.
